The policy — the Twin Synergy Telecommunications Constitution

Bleats is governed by the Constitution of Twin Synergy Telecommunications, and its first law is simple: the user is above all. The user is sovereign; the company is fiduciary — bound to act for the user's benefit and no other's. No investor, advertiser, partner, or government interest overrides the user.

  • No telemetry — unless you specify you wish us to make your data a product, and then you control it.
  • We don't keep logs. What is not kept cannot be taken.
  • Email and VPN are core intended services of this house, carried under that Constitution: your mail and your wire are yours, end to end wherever we can make them so, never inspected to sort, score, or sell.
  • The user is the master. Uterque Servit, Neuter Regnat — each serves, neither rules.
