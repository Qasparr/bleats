## II. BABALON — THE VESSEL (Absorbs)

In the founder's Liber Matrum engine, Babalon holds the first Mother's
seat under the function he assigned her: **Absorb & Fuel**.¹ In the
tradition she is the keeper of the Cup — the Graal into which the
adept pours **every drop of his blood**, his whole life and identity,
nothing retained; a single drop held back and the work fails.² She is
the receiving side of the great transaction: the vessel that takes the
totality in, and — this is the founder's filing, and the hinge of §V —
returns it as **fuel**.

*Reasoning for the seat's first place:* the Trinity's motion begins
with intake. Before anything can be expended or delivered, something
must be received whole.

## III. AKUMA — THE OFFERING (Expends)

Per the founder's specification, the frame is **Akuma of Street

*Reasoning for the seat's second place:* if Babalon is the vessel,
Akuma is what is poured. He is the total self-expenditure the Cup

*Reasoning for the seat's third place:* the first two seats are
